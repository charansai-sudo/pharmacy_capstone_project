# Reliability Checklist

## 1. Safety Check

Safety check: No personally identifiable customer data appears in the memo or narrative; only region-level aggregate information is used.

## 2. Validation

Validation: The Guntur April 2026 sales, May 2026 sales, and April→May change were checked against the Part 2 SQL-derived metrics.

## 3. Critique / Refine

Critique/refine: The memo was reviewed to ensure that dataset-derived facts are separated from hypotheses and that no unsupported external market explanations are presented as facts.

## 4. Human Sign-off

Human sign-off: The final memo has been reviewed by the human reviewer and is approved for downstream use.
